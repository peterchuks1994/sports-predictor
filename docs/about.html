About Matchday Calls

Matchday Calls is a free, personal hobby project built to explore how far a simple prediction model can be pushed using only free, public football data. It's not a company, not a paid product, and not affiliated with any betting operator.

How the predictions work

Every match is analyzed across three markets — Double Chance, Over/Under 2.5 Goals, and Both Teams to Score — using a blend of:

ELO ratings: each team carries a running skill rating that updates after every result, weighted by the strength of the opponent they played.

Live league standings: current league position and points are factored in alongside form, so a team's whole season matters, not just their last few games.

Recent form and scoring data: each team's last five results, goals scored, and goals conceded feed a Poisson goal model to estimate the likely number of goals in a match.

Head-to-head history: where one side has a clear historical edge over the specific opponent, that nudges the prediction slightly.

For each match, the app compares all three markets and highlights whichever one shows the strongest signal above what would be true by chance alone — rather than forcing a confident-sounding pick every time.

How accurate is it?

Genuinely honest answer: football is hard to predict, and no model — free or professional — gets this right every time. The site tracks its own real accuracy openly on the home page as predictions resolve, rather than only showing the picks that turned out right.

Data source

All football data comes from football-data.org's free public tier, covering major European leagues including the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, and others.

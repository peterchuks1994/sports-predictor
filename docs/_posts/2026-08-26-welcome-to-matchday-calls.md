Matchday Calls started as a simple idea: could a genuinely transparent, free prediction model be built using only public football data, without pretending to know more than it does?

Every prediction on this site is generated by a model that blends ELO ratings, live league standings, recent form, and head-to-head history. Rather than always forcing a match-result pick, it looks across three markets — Double Chance, Over/Under 2.5 Goals, and Both Teams to Score — and highlights whichever one shows the clearest signal for that specific match.

The accuracy dashboard on the home page isn't for show — it updates automatically as matches resolve, showing the real hit rate for each market. No cherry-picking, no hiding the misses.

More posts on the methodology, weekly recaps, and interesting matchups will show up here over time.
